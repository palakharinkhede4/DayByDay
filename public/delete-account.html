Account Deletion Request

If you no longer wish to use DayByDay, you can delete your account directly inside the mobile application by navigating to Settings > Delete Account.

If you have already uninstalled the app and wish to have your account and all associated data permanently deleted, please send an email request to our support team.

To request deletion:

  1. Send an email to: support@daybypalak.duckdns.org
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request
  3. Include your username in the body of the email so we can locate your account.

We will process your request and permanently delete your data within 7 business days.
